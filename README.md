### Heyo

I’m a Senior Bioinformatics Research Scientist in Suzanne Baker’s lab in
the Department of Developmental Neurobiology at St. Jude Children’s
Research Hospital. We study oncohistone mutations in the context of
pediatric high grade gliomas.

I am/have been involved in a handful of Bioconductor/CRAN R packages, including:

- [VizModules](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/VizModules/index.html) (developer, maintainer),
- [CRISPRball](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/CRISPRball.html) (developer, maintainer),
- [dittoSeq](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/dittoSeq.html) (contributor),
- [dittoViz](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dittoViz/index.html) (contributor),
- [SingleR](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/SingleR.html) (contributor),
- [escape](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/escape.html) (contributor),
- [celldex](http://bioconductor.org/packages/release/data/experiment/html/celldex.html) (contributor),
- [EnhancedVolcano](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/EnhancedVolcano.html) (maintainer),
- [PCAtools](https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/PCAtools.html) (maintainer)

On occasion, I develop python packages, including:

- [STRprofiler](https://pypi.org/project/strprofiler/) (developer, maintainer)

PRs welcome.
